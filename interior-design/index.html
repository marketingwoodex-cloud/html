Design by space

Homes and commercial interiors need different answers.

Both follow one clear process: understand, plan, select, visualize and document.

Residential

Comfort, storage and personal routines guide every room.

  • Kitchens and bedrooms
  • Living and dining rooms
  • Home offices and kids rooms
  • Basements and complete homes

Commercial

Customer flow, team needs and brand standards shape the design.

  • Offices and workplaces
  • Retail and showrooms
  • Restaurants and hospitality
  • Healthcare interiors

Style, without a formula

Six directions. One should feel like yours.

From brief to design package

Five decisions, made in the right order.

Each step resolves the next. That keeps late changes and site surprises to a minimum.

Consultation

We understand the space, routines, taste, budget and timeline.

Site Measurement

Walls, openings, services and existing elements are recorded accurately.

Concept & Layout

Furniture, circulation, storage and focal points establish the plan.

Materials & Furniture

Colours, finishes, lighting, curtains and furniture are coordinated.

Final Package & 3D

Drawings, specifications and renders show exactly what will be built.
