What is kitchen design?

Planning the room before building it.

Kitchen design is the practical and visual planning of the room. It starts with the sink, stove and refrigerator work triangle, then resolves the layout, modular or custom cabinetry, storage, countertops and appliance positions.

It also brings lighting, ventilation, materials and 3D visualization into one coordinated plan. Kitchen design sets the decisions. Renovation is the execution of those decisions on site.

Kitchen layouts we design

A clear plan for every footprint.

The right layout depends on room width, openings, circulation, utilities and the number of people using the kitchen.

CORNER

L-Shaped

Open corners and efficient movement for compact or connected living spaces.

PARALLEL

Parallel / Galley

Two productive runs that keep storage, prep and cooking close.

THREE SIDES

U-Shaped

Generous work surface and storage across three connected sides.

CENTRAL

Island

A central zone for preparation, seating, storage or cooking.

LINEAR

One-Wall

A disciplined linear plan for studios, apartments and narrow rooms.

PENINSULA

G-Shaped / Peninsula

Extra counter space and zoning without a fully detached island.

Modular vs custom kitchen

Choose the build method that fits.

Both can deliver a refined kitchen. The difference is how the cabinets are engineered, fitted and installed.

SYSTEM

Modular kitchen

Pre-engineered modules support faster installation, consistent quality and efficient planning for standard layouts.

BESPOKE

Custom kitchen

Bespoke joinery is measured and made for a tailored fit, irregular spaces or very specific functional needs.
