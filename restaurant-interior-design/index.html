Our Design Process

Five focused stages take the restaurant from brief to an approved design package.

01

Consultation & Requirements

We discuss the cuisine, guests, cover target, service style, budget and opening plan.

02

Site Measurement

We record the floor, access, services, kitchen needs and existing conditions.

03

Concept & Layout Design

Dining, bar, kitchen and guest movement are tested as one working plan.

04

Material & Furniture Selection

Durable finishes, tables, seating, lighting and décor are coordinated.

05

Final Design Package & 3D Visualization
