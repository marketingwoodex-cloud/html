Design scope

What the architecture covers

Create a facility where patients, staff and services move safely. Clinical flow, infection control and medical infrastructure shape every planning decision.

01Patient and staff flowSeparate public, clinical, staff and service movement where the brief requires it.
02Clinical zoningPlace consultation, treatment, diagnostic and support rooms around workflow.
03Infection controlPlan clean and dirty routes, handwashing and material transitions early.
04Accessibility and privacySupport dignified access, clear wayfinding and confidential care.
05Medical infrastructureReserve space for equipment, gases, backup power and specialist services.
06Compliance drawingsCoordinate the architectural package around applicable healthcare requirements.

Eight-step process

From brief to buildable drawings

Each stage closes the important decisions before the next one begins.

01

Brief and site analysis

Confirm the project brief, review the site and record the constraints that shape the design.

02

Concept planning

Test layout, zoning, access and movement before the architectural form is fixed.

03

Form and 3D studies

Develop the exterior character and review key views, proportions and materials.

04

Design development

Resolve dimensions, openings, levels, materials and the key technical decisions.

05

Working drawings

Prepare coordinated plans, sections, elevations and the agreed construction details.

06

Authority submission

Develop the required architectural set around the relevant approval process.

07

Medical and building systems

Align equipment, medical gases, MEP, fire, accessibility and infection-control needs.

08

Final handover

Issue the agreed drawing package for pricing, coordination and construction.
