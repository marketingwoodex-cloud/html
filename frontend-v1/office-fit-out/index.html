From shell to working office

What Is Office Fit-Out?

It is the work that turns an empty commercial space into an office your team can use.

A typical shell has concrete surfaces and basic utility points. Fit-out adds the rooms, finishes, services and furniture that make it practical. Aftercare terms are written into your contract before work starts.

Office Fit-Out Process, Step by Step

Eight practical stages, with scope and price agreed before site work begins.

Site survey

We measure the space and review existing structure, services and access.

Layout planning

Rooms, workstations and circulation are set around your team.

BOQ and price

Every agreed item, milestone and date is written into the scope.

Material selection

You approve partition, ceiling, flooring, lighting and joinery finishes.

Site execution

All trades are sequenced and supervised by the Woodex project team.

Furniture install

Joinery, workstations, seating and storage are fitted into place.

Snagging

We test services and correct every item before completion.

Handover

The office is cleaned, tested and ready for your team to move in.

Typical timeline: 3 to 6 weeks for a 1,000 to 3,000 sq. ft. office, subject to scope, materials and building access.
