How we work

Four clear stages from first call to handover

Every project follows the same accountable process, so you always know what happens next, who owns it and what it costs.

  1. Brief & survey

    We visit, measure and record how the space must work, your budget and your timeline.

  2. Design & 3D

    Layouts, materials and lighting resolved, then shown in photoreal 3D before you commit.

  3. Build & make

    Site trades and joinery teams deliver to one programme and one set of drawings.

  4. Handover

    Snagging, final finishes and a walkthrough, with the space ready to use on the agreed date.
