What Is Home Refurbishment?

Home refurbishment improves an existing interior while keeping the main structure. Floors, wall finishes, lighting, storage, fixtures and furniture can all be updated around a better room plan.

It is more focused than a complete home redesign and lighter than architectural work. The aim is to make the home feel renewed without rebuilding every room or changing the building itself.

The final scope can be as simple as a room refresh or as broad as a coordinated whole-home upgrade.

Refurbishment vs Full Redesign

Both improve the home. The difference is how much of the existing interior stays in place.

Focused upgrade

Refurbishment

Upgrade what exists with selected finish, fixture and layout changes. It is faster, costs less and causes less disruption.

Complete overhaul

Complete Redesign

Rework the full interior direction with major layout changes, a broader design scope and a higher budget.
