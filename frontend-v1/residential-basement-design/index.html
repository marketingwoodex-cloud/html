What Is Basement Design?

Basement design turns an unused or underused lower level into a functional, beautiful room. The right plan starts with the basement itself, not just the look you want.

Moisture, limited daylight, ventilation and structural conditions all need to be understood first. Once the space is sound, we plan the layout for an entertainment room, home theater, gym, guest room, playroom, office or bar lounge.
