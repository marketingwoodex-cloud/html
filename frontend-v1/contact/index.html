FAQ

Common questions

Where is the Woodex studio?

M-71, Zainab Tower, Model Town Link Road, Lahore. Open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 6:30 pm. Closed on Sunday.

What is the quickest way to reach Woodex?

Call or WhatsApp +92 322 4000768, email info@woodex.com.pk, or book a visit online.

What should I include in my first message?

Your area or city, the type and size of the space, what you want to change, and a few photos or plans if you have them. This helps the designer prepare before the first call.

Can a designer visit my site?

Yes. Choose a time on the booking page and the visit is confirmed with you on WhatsApp during working hours.
