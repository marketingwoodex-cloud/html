Our Architecture Process

Eight clear stages from the first brief to the final construction package.

Typical timeline: 4–12 weeks, depending on project size and authority processing.

Brief & Site Survey

We understand the requirements, visit the site, take measurements and check the relevant zoning and bylaws.

Site Survey

Boundaries, levels, access, utilities and neighbouring structures are recorded in detail.

Concept Design, 2D + 3D

Floor plans, elevations and 3D views establish the design. Two revision rounds are included.

Design Development

Dimensions, materials, structural grids and service zones are technically resolved.

Working Drawings

Plans, sections, elevations and details are prepared in AutoCAD or Revit.

Authority Submission

The required drawing set is prepared and coordinated with the relevant authority.

Structural Coordination

We align the architecture with the structural engineering team for buildability.

Final Handover

The complete construction-ready drawing package is issued to the client or contractor.
