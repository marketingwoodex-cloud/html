What Is Office Interior Design?

Office interior design shapes a workplace so it functions well, reflects the brand and supports the people using it. It starts with understanding how the business works, how teams connect and what clients should experience on arrival.

The design package is the plan: layouts, 3D visuals, material choices and technical drawings. Fit-out is the physical execution. Woodex offers both separately or as one coordinated design and build project.
