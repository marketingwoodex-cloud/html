How the estimate works

Is this the final price?

No. It is a starting range built from our recent projects. The final figure comes after a site visit, drawings review and an itemised BOQ, the same format we have used for years.

What moves the price most?

Finish level and scope. A premium material palette, imported fittings or heavy civil work can move a project from the lower end of the range to the upper end.

Do you charge for the detailed quotation?

The site visit and detailed quotation are free for serious enquiries within Lahore. Outstation projects are quoted case by case.
