What Is Bedroom Design?

Bedroom design brings rest, function and beauty into one private space. It considers where the bed sits, how you move, what you need to store and how the room feels at night.

Woodex designs master bedrooms, guest rooms and small bedrooms. Kids rooms have their own focused service on our Kids Room Design page.

What We Deliver

A complete bedroom plan, from the first furniture position to the final layer of light and texture.

Layout Planning & Bed Placement

We place the bed around doors, windows and easy circulation.

Master Suite Design

Sleeping, sitting, wardrobe and dressing areas planned as one suite.

Wardrobes & Storage

Sliding or swing doors with practical internal organisation.

Lighting Design

Ambient, bedside and accent lighting with useful dimmer controls.

Colour Scheme & Materials

Restful palettes, wall finishes and flooring chosen together.

Furniture Layout

Bed, bedside tables and seating balanced with clear movement.

Decorative Elements

Art, mirrors, rugs and accessories that add character without clutter.

Small Bedroom Solutions

Under-bed storage, wall-mounted pieces, mirrors and lighter colours.
