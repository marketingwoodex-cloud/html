A controlled programme

12 steps from brief to handover

Decisions are approved in sequence so the site team can build with fewer changes and clearer accountability.

01

Brief and survey

Confirm goals, site conditions and what is technically possible.

02

Concept design

Set the spatial character, palette and material direction.

03

Space planning

Resolve layouts, furniture positions and circulation.

04

3D visualization

Review and approve the finished space on screen.

05

Material specification

Select every finish, fixture and key fitting.

06

BOQ and cost plan

Approve an itemized budget before contract.

07

Contract and mobilisation

Fix milestones, payment schedule and programme.

08

Strip-out and prep

Remove agreed elements and prepare the site.

09

MEP first-fix

Install power, plumbing, HVAC and data infrastructure.

10

Finishes and furniture

Build walls, ceilings, floors, joinery and furniture.

11

Quality inspection

Correct snags, re-inspect and deep clean.

12

Formal handover

Issue documentation and release the finished space.
