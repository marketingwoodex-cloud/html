A controlled programme

7 steps from brief to handover

Decisions are approved in sequence so the site team can build with fewer changes and clearer accountability.

01

Survey

Assess the shell, utilities, access and building rules.

02

Plan

Resolve business zones, circulation and built-in elements.

03

Agree itemized quantities, price, milestones and timeline.

04

Select

Approve materials, fixtures, finishes and samples.

05

Build

06

Install

07

Handover
