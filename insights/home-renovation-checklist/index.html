Renovation

A calm home renovation checklist

Survey first, freeze scope, hold a buffer, sequence the trades. A calm renovation is a planned one.

Category
Renovation
Read time
5 min read
Studio
Woodex Studio

Survey first

Every renovation starts with a proper survey. Structure, plumbing, electrical load and damp are checked before the design is drawn.

Surprises found on paper cost a conversation. Surprises found mid-build cost money and weeks.

Freeze scope, hold a buffer

Decide the full scope and freeze it before work begins. Changes mid-build are the main reason renovations overrun.

Hold a buffer of 10 to 15 percent of the budget for what the walls reveal. If you do not spend it, it is yours.

Sequence the trades

Renovations run in a fixed order: demolition and civil work, plumbing and electrical, then joinery, paint and finishing. Each trade needs the one before it finished.

Rushing the sequence creates rework. A realistic programme agreed upfront keeps everyone honest.

Living through it

Decide early whether you stay or move out. Living through a renovation saves rent but slows work and tests patience.

If you stay, agree on dust control, working hours and which rooms stay usable. Put it in writing. See our residential renovation service for how we manage occupied homes.

The short version.

  • Survey before design: Know the bones of the house before drawing anything.
  • Freeze and buffer: Locked scope plus a 10 to 15 percent buffer keeps budgets calm.
  • Respect the sequence: Civil, services, joinery, finishes. In that order.
