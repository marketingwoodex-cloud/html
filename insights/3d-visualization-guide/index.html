Stills, walkthroughs and 360s

Stills are photorealistic views of key rooms, the fastest way to judge a design. Walkthroughs are short films that move through the space, showing how rooms connect.

360 views let you look around from fixed points on your phone. Together they remove the guesswork that flat drawings leave behind.

Two revision rounds, 4K and up

Every project includes 2 structured revision rounds after the first views are issued. You review, we revise, and the design converges without endless loops.

Delivery is 4K resolution and above, sharp enough to judge materials, shadows and proportions properly.

Decisions are cheaper in 3D

Changing a wall colour in a render costs almost nothing. Changing it on site costs paint, labour and days. The same goes for tile layouts, joinery details and lighting positions.

Clients who lock decisions in 3D see fewer variations during build. Fewer variations mean calmer timelines and steadier budgets.

The short version.

  • Three formats: Stills for decisions, walkthroughs for flow, 360s for exploring on your phone.
  • 2 rounds included: Structured revisions keep the design converging instead of drifting.
  • Cheaper to change pixels: Every decision locked in 3D is a variation avoided on site.
