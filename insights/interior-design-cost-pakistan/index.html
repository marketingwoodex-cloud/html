What interior design costs in Pakistan

Know the typical cost bands for design and build in Pakistan, and what moves a number up or down.

Category
Cost guide
Read time
6 min read
Studio
Woodex Studio

Design only

Design-only fees in Pakistan usually fall between PKR 100 and 350 per square foot. That covers space planning, elevations, a material palette and joinery drawings.

It does not include construction. Clients who already have a contractor use this service to lock the design before spending on site.

Build costs by space

A single room refresh usually lands between PKR 3 and 8 lakh, depending on joinery and finishes. A full home turnkey build typically ranges from PKR 2,500 to 5,500 per square foot.

Office fit out commonly sits between PKR 2,000 and 4,500 per square foot. These are rough guidance bands, not quotes. A site visit and drawings turn them into real numbers.

What moves the number

Four things drive most of the variance. Scope, meaning how much of the space is touched. Material grade, from local laminates to imported surfaces. Joinery volume, since custom wardrobes and kitchens are labour heavy.

And site condition: civil repairs, plumbing and electrical upgrades add cost before any finishing begins. The city matters too, as labour and material rates shift between Lahore, Islamabad and Karachi.

What a BOQ is, and why we share it

A BOQ is a bill of quantities. Every item in the design is listed with its quantity and its rate, so the total is built line by line instead of guessed.

We share quantities before build so you approve numbers, not promises. When another firm quotes without a BOQ, compare scopes carefully before comparing prices.

The short version.

  • Guidance, not quotes: Published ranges help you plan. A survey and drawings give you the real number.
  • Joinery moves the budget: Custom kitchens, wardrobes and counters are where most cost decisions live.
  • Ask for a BOQ: Quantities with rates turn a quote into a plan you can hold someone to.
