What We Design

Eight connected details shape the coffee service, guest comfort and street presence.

01

Counter & Bar Design

A welcoming service point planned around equipment, display and staff movement.

02

Seating Layout

Indoor, window and outdoor seats balanced with clear guest and service routes.

03

Lighting & Ambience

Warm layers of light for mornings, late evenings and flattering table settings.

04

Brand Styling & Décor

Colour, materials, furniture and details that make the brand feel at home.

05

Menu Board & Graphics

Clear menu presentation and graphic touchpoints placed where guests need them.

06

Kitchenette & Prep Area

A practical back counter for prep, storage, washing and daily service flow.

07

Facade & Signage

A recognisable shopfront that helps the café stand out from the street.

08

Acoustic Comfort

Materials and layout choices that soften noise without losing the café buzz.

Our Design Process

Five focused stages take the café from first conversation to an approved design package.

01

Consultation & Requirements

We discuss the coffee offer, guest profile, service style, budget and opening plan.

02

Site Measurement

We record the floor, frontage, services, equipment needs and existing conditions.

03

Concept & Layout Design

Counter, seating, prep and customer movement come together in one clear plan.

04

Material & Furniture Selection

Durable finishes, tables, seating, lighting and brand details are coordinated.

05

Final Design Package & 3D Visualization

Drawings and 3D views prepare the café for confident pricing and execution.
