What Is 3D Visualization?

Why it matters

Decisions made on screen cost nothing to change.

Most interior disputes come from a gap between what a client imagined and what was drawn. Visualisation closes that gap. You see the finished ceiling, the veneer direction, the lighting temperature and the furniture layout while everything is still editable.

Because our render team sits beside our design and site teams, what you approve is what we build — no reinterpretation between image and installation.
