What Is a Complete Home Redesign?

Every room is handled as one project, by one team and under one contract. A unified design language connects the palette, materials, lighting and built details across the home.

It is more considered and efficient than commissioning rooms separately. Work can be phased room by room, so clients can often remain at home while each area is completed.

The redesign covers the built space. Furniture-inclusive options are also available by contacting Woodex.
