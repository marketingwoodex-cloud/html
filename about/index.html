About the studio

Design and build, held to one standard

Woodex Interior was founded in Lahore in 2016. We plan, design and deliver offices, commercial spaces and premium homes across Pakistan with one accountable team.

Who we are

A decade of design, delivered on site.

Founded in 2016 by Imtiaz Ahmad, Woodex Interior is a Lahore-based design-and-build company working across corporate offices, retail, hospitality, healthcare and premium residential projects. With more than 200 completed spaces, our work is shaped as much by site experience as by drawing boards.

Design, visualization, joinery and site delivery sit inside one studio, supported by Woodex 3D Studio™ and Woodex Furniture™. That means fewer handovers, clearer accountability and a finished space that matches what was approved.
