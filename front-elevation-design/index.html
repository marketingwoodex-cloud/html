Eight-step process

From brief to buildable drawings

Each stage closes the important decisions before the next one begins.

01

Brief and site analysis

Confirm the project brief, review the site and record the constraints that shape the design.

02

Concept planning

Test layout, zoning, access and movement before the architectural form is fixed.

03

Form and 3D studies

Develop the exterior character and review key views, proportions and materials.

04

Design development

Resolve dimensions, openings, levels, materials and the key technical decisions.

05

Working drawings

Prepare coordinated plans, sections, elevations and the agreed construction details.

06

Authority submission

Develop the required architectural set around the relevant approval process.

07

Plan and structure check

Review the supplied floor plans and structural positions before fixing facade elements.

08

Final handover

Issue the agreed drawing package for pricing, coordination and construction.
