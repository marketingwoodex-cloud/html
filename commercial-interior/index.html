The Commercial Design Process

Five practical stages move the brief from business requirements to an approved, build-ready design.

1

Consultation & Requirements

We define the brand, customer experience, staff needs, budget and operational priorities.

2

Site Measurement

We record dimensions, services, access and any landlord or compliance conditions.

3

Concept & Layout Design

Footfall flow, staff workflow and the visual direction are tested together.

4

Material & Furniture Selection

Durable finishes, lighting and furniture are coordinated with the brand and daily use.

5

Final Design Package & 3D Visualization

Approved visuals and technical drawings prepare the space for pricing and execution.
